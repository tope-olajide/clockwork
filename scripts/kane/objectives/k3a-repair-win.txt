Open the Neon Runner demo project with the demoReset query parameter and stay in this one browser session from start to finish; do not reload, reset, or start a second run. Open the agent drawer and switch it to Build mode before sending anything; Ask is read-only and cannot apply changes. Send exactly: "One coin in Neon Runner cannot be collected. Inspect the scene, find out why, and move it somewhere reachable." If a command is rejected, read the reported expected arguments, fix them, and send the corrected change instead of the same batch again. Wait until the change summary says the change was applied. Press Play and confirm the stage loads. In Play, click the stage to the right of the player and hold the pointer there until the player stops, then click further right, repeating until the COINS counter reads 3 and the win message appears. Report the coin counter at the end and stop Play.

Open the Neon Runner demo project with the demoReset query parameter. Open the agent drawer and switch it to Build mode before sending anything; Ask is read-only and cannot apply changes. Send: "One coin in Neon Runner cannot be collected. Inspect the scene, find out why, and move it somewhere reachable." Wait for the applied change and its change summary. Press Play. In Play, click the stage to the right of the player, wait for the player to stop, then click further right, repeating until the COINS counter reads 3 and the win message appears. Stop Play and press Undo once to confirm the change is one undo entry, then redo it.

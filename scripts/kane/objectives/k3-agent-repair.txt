Open the Neon Runner demo project with the demoReset query parameter. Ask the agent: "One coin in Neon Runner cannot be collected. Inspect the scene, find out why, and move it somewhere reachable." Wait for the change summary. Press Play. In Play, click the stage to the right of the player and wait for the player to run to that point, then click further right again, repeating until the COINS counter reads 3 and the win message appears. Stop Play and press Undo once to confirm the change is one undo entry, then redo it.

# Session: slice-b-space-invaders

## Step 1
Go to http://localhost:5173 and create a project from the Space Invaders game starter named Slice B Invaders. Open the project in the editor and click Play. Verify the Play screen shows SCORE, SHIPS and INVADERS with the game canvas. Click the game canvas, press and hold D briefly to move the defender, and press and hold Space briefly to fire at the formation. Verify the game is still running and the SHIPS and INVADERS HUD labels remain visible. Press R to restart and verify the running Play screen shows SCORE, SHIPS and INVADERS again. Check the browser console and verify there are no runtime errors.
